Habit Garden 🌱

A habit tracker that turns your routines into a garden. Every day you keep a habit, you water its plant. Keep the streak going and it grows from a seed into a flower. Miss a day and it starts again.

  • 6 growth stages tied to your streak, from Seed to Blooming at 20+ days
  • Detects a new day on its own and resets missed streaks
  • A daily progress bar for how much of the garden you've watered
  • Saves to your browser, so there's no login or database
  • Hand-coded inline SVG plants, works on mobile

Built for Smart India Hackathon problem SIH26027 (Ministry of Railways). It plans the daily maintenance blocks on railway sections, finding the fewest safe blocks that clear the most critical work while delaying trains the least.
